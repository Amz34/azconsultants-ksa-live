FAQs

The questions that come up first

Do we need a Saudi entity to work with you?

No. Advisory mandates can start immediately under a services agreement. For work that must be awarded to a licensed local entity, we deliver through a registered Saudi partner and stay accountable for the technical scope.

Can you help with Etimad registration and portal work?

Yes. We prepare the supplier file, check the activity classification, and handle the document side of submissions. The portal account itself stays in your name.

What does a first engagement cost?

We do not publish a rate card, because the same deliverable costs differently in different scopes, so we quote every mandate at our best market price for that work. Ask us for the number and it reaches you in writing, usually within one working day. Every proposal states the deliverable, the date and the price before work starts.
