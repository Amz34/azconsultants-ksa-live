KSA government tender board

Government tenders published on the national procurement portal, with the project detail, the deadlines and a direct link to the official page. Closing dates are shown exactly as published; once a date passes the entry stays on the board and is marked as expired, so the record remains readable. The board is split into competitions and consultancy, and procurement of items and related contracts.

Competitions and consultancy

Public competitions whose scope sits closest to advisory work: services, operations and maintenance, information technology, studies and supervision.

Procurement of items and related contracts

Supply scopes published on the same portal: goods, equipment, spare parts and materials. Useful where a client needs a supply partner rather than a services partner.

How to read this board. Entries summarise what the portal publishes publicly; the official documents on Etimad always take precedence. Document values, guarantees and submission rules are set by the publishing entity. Both sections carry live entries, newest first; direct purchases appear only where the scope falls inside our service lines or supplies goods. Entries marked as picked for our service lines sit closest to our advisory work and are not a recommendation to bid.
